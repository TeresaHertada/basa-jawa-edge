BASA-JAWA Edge learning materials and classifier export

Dataset: JavaneseHonorifics/Unggah-Ungguh, released translation and conversation datasets.
Source: https://huggingface.co/datasets/JavaneseHonorifics/Unggah-Ungguh

A. F. Aji, and D. T. Wijaya. Do Language Models Understand Honorific Systems
in Javanese? Proceedings of ACL, Volume 1, 2025, pages 26732-26754.
DOI: 10.18653/v1/2025.acl-long.1296
Paper: https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.1296/

Adaptations in this application: CSV rows were converted to local JSON for
example search, source-label quizzes, and dialogue display. The classifier
was trained on a grouped training partition and exported to browser weights.
Original example text and source speech-level labels are retained. The local
example search uses word/phrase matching. Predictions are experimental and
are not an endorsement or contextual politeness certification by the source authors.

This package is prepared for noncommercial educational demonstration.
The dataset licence and attribution remain attached to the source materials.
